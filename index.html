About AI safety Biology Writing

Hi, I’m Antoine. I’m working on mechanistic interpretability as a MATS scholar. My current research goal is to identify the mechanisms that LLMs use to process information, by extracting them directly from the weights.

Before that, I did a PhD in biophysics at Institut Pasteur, building CRISPR tools to study the morphogenesis of bacteria, and a postdoc at Harvard Medical School, where I built machines that keep bacteria growing continuously for years, so we can see evolution happen in real time (120,000+ generations so far). I also write Telescopic Turnip, a blog about science, progress and the strange rules that shape daily life.

The best way to reach out is by email, or by DM on the Open Source Mech Interp Slack. If you're working on an exciting research project, I'd love to hear about it!
